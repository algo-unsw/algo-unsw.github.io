Approximation algorithms cope with computationally intractable problems by producing solutions that are guaranteed to be not too far from optimal.
Constant-factor approximation algorithms find solutions within a constant factor of the optimal solution.
Approximation schemes allow to adjust the approximation factor arbitrarily close to 1 at the expense of slower running times.
